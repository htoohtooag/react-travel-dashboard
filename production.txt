=> After Deploy

1. update the "VITE_BASE_URL" with your domain 

2. Add Your Custom Domain to Appwrite
    - Open the Appwrite Console and select your project.
    - Find Add Platform → choose "Web".
    - For Hostname, enter the exact domain without the protocol: react-travel-dashboard.vercel.app.
    - Click "Create".

3. Add Your Google Cloud Console Settings ("Adding client")
    - Go to the Google Cloud Console and select your project. ("Search Google auth platform in browser")
    - go to "clients"
    - In the "Authorized JavaScript origins" section, ensure you have both of these entries:
        - Add your domain
