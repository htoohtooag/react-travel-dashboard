        @syncfusion/ej2-react-dropdowns

    => Make ssr for syncfusion (server side rendering) 
        ssr: {
        noExternal: [/@syncfusion/]
        } 
        ❝ DO NOT treat @syncfusion/* as external Instead, bundle it and transform it before running SSR ❞
